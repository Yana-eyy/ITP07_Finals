Frequently Asked Questions

Simply fill out the booking form above with your departure point, destination, preferred date and time, and number of passengers. Click "Search Available Boats" to see available options and confirm your booking.

We accept cash payments and digital payments through GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfers. Payment is collected at the departure dock.

Yes, you can cancel up to 30 minutes before your scheduled departure with a full refund, or reschedule for a small fee. Contact our support team for assistance.

All boats are regularly inspected, equipped with life jackets, and operated by trained captains. We follow strict safety regulations set by maritime authorities.

Driver FAQ

Open your "My Trips" dashboard, review the trip details, and click "Accept" on the trip card. The rider will be notified and the trip will be marked as assigned to you.

Use the "Report Issue" button within the active trip card. Provide a short description and any photos if needed. Our operations team will receive the report and respond accordingly.

Ensure life jackets are available, check fuel and engine status, confirm passenger count, and verify weather conditions. Mark the pre-departure checklist as complete before leaving the dock.

Driver earnings are calculated per completed trip and reconciled weekly. For payment disputes, contact support with trip details and we will investigate.
